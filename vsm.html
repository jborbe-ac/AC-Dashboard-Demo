VSM Transformation Story

Value-Add Ratio
34% → 96%
NVA Time
23d → 2d

In early 2026, AC HHS conducted a value stream mapping exercise across the entire OASIS-to-billing workflow. The audit revealed a single redundant step, a fragmented QA process spread across multiple vendors, and a near-complete absence of daily pipeline visibility — almost a third of the cycle time was spent waiting between handoffs.

The post-redesign workflow is built around a tighter vendor structure (clinician → coding vendor → QA/POC vendor → billing vendor), a single source of truth in this dashboard, and daily review cadence. The lead time is now intentionally aligned to the 30-day care cycle — longer in total clock time, but with non-value-added wait collapsed from ~23 days down to ~2.

What Changed

Structural changes

CM Audit step eliminated. The earlier process inserted a Care Management audit between Coding and QA1; the audit was redundant with QA1's same scope of review. Removing it cut roughly 4–6 days of waiting per chart.

QA consolidated under one vendor. Previously, three different vendors touched the QA, POC creation, and POC faxing steps. The work is now consolidated under a single QA/POC vendor with one queue, one SLA, and one point of contact.

Daily pipeline visibility. Each step's queue is now tracked in real time on this dashboard, so exceptions surface the day they happen rather than at month-end review.

Remaining gaps

Two stages are still missing target as of April 2026:

• QA1 Review — running at 4.67d against a 2d target. Capacity on the QA vendor side is the constraint; cross-training a second reviewer is in progress.

• POC Completion — running at 3.5d against a 2d target. Closely tied to QA1 throughput — many POCs sit waiting because the chart is still upstream in QA1.

Closing both gaps would put the agency at 100% target attainment across the full pipeline.
