What this prototype demonstrates

The operational story

Home health agencies juggle a tightly choreographed sequence — clinician documentation, coding, QA review, plan of care, physician signature, and billing. Each step has a CMS-driven target. Each delay compounds into late billing, lost revenue, and lower outcome scores. This dashboard turns that workflow from a spreadsheet exercise into a live operating picture.

The platform behind it

The live application is a Streamlit app backed by Google Sheets, with a Claude-powered analyst (“Dasha”) grounded in the same data. Aggregations refresh on every page load; status pills, urgency cues, and CMS benchmarks are computed against rolling 12-month windows. This static prototype mirrors the visual system without exposing any PHI.
